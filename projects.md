## In Progress
*Primary research focus and active modeling.*

**[Capacity accreditation. Is the grid's reliability accounting honest?](/research/capacity/)**
Did accredited capacity values predict actual delivery during the tightest hours? Testing PJM/MISO accreditation values vs. EIA-923/930 hourly generation during Winter Storm Elliott, and exploring the "death spiral" of marginal ELCC.

**[Interconnection queues. Can clean capacity actually arrive?](/research/queues/)**
Survival analysis of the queue using LBNL's Queued Up data. Examining what predicts completion vs. withdrawal, and whether FERC Order 2023 changed survival dynamics.
